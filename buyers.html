Buying a home

First home or your next one, we walk with you from the first showing to closing day.

How it works

  1. Talk with us

    We start with a conversation. What you want, where you want to be, and what fits your budget.

  2. Get pre-approved

    A pre-approval shows sellers you are serious. We can share the names of local lenders our clients have used, and you pick who you work with.

  3. Tour homes

    We set up showings, point out what we see, and give you honest opinions. You can also search on your own anytime.

  4. Make an offer

    We look at recent sales nearby, help you decide on a price, and negotiate on your behalf.

  5. Inspections to closing

    We keep inspections, appraisal, and paperwork on track, and keep you posted the whole way.

Before we tour homes

We will walk you through a short written buyer agreement. It spells out what we do for you and how we are paid. No surprises.
