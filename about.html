Our story

A Good Life Team helps people buy and sell homes across Florida's Space Coast.

We're Jason and Kari Bollinger

We built this team together.

Jason's family has worked at Kennedy Space Center for three generations. Kari has taught students in Titusville and Port St. John for more than 20 years.

We've called Brevard home for over 40 years, and we're raising our three kids here. When we're not working with clients, you'll find us on the beach or at one of our kids' soccer games, cheer competitions, or theater shows.

We started A Good Life Team because we love serving people and we love this community. Helping a family find the right home here is one of the best ways we know to do both.
